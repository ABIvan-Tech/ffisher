FFISHER Data Safety Summary

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Important: This is a plain-language summary, not the official Google Play Data safety form. Google Play declarations must be completed and kept current for the final released build, including all third-party SDK behavior.

At a glance

FFISHER has no user accounts, no FFISHER cloud profile, and no FFISHER-operated backend in the current release. The Android build includes Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config, Google Mobile Ads, and Google UMP. Their SDKs may process data according to their configuration, policies, and consent state.

Public data sources

FFISHER retrieves public information from the Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI), the Finnish Environment Institute (SYKE), Kalastusrajoitus.fi, and the National Land Survey of Finland. These sources provide weather forecasts, water-body and hydrology information, map tiles, and fishing-restriction information.

Not requested by the current release

  • user accounts or account deletion;
  • contacts;
  • microphone;
  • photo-library access;
  • background location; and
  • sale of personal data by FFISHER.

Retention, deletion and consent

FFISHER does not currently provide an account or cloud database containing a user profile. Local app data can generally be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the App. Firebase, Google advertising services, FMI, SYKE, map providers, and other external services may retain technical or request data under their own policies. Android advertising consent is requested through Google UMP where required.
